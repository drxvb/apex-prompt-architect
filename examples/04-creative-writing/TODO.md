## What this example should demonstrate
- D2 catches: no genre, no length, no perspective, no constraints — extremely open-ended
- D3 should ADD constraint without killing creativity: genre selector, word range, POV choice, "show don't tell" guideline
- Three platforms diverge sharply here: Claude can carry rich constraint scaffolding without dampening output; MiniMax variant should be radically minimal to let the model breathe

## Pedagogical value
- Shows that more scaffolding ≠ better output for creative tasks
- Shows platform-fit tradeoff (Claude vs MiniMax is sharpest here)
